# Proposal: NordFrame Warehouse Operations Portal

**Submitted by:** Syncro Logistics Software (fictional)
**Variant: GOOD (Score: 3.5/5) — Solid functional fit, clear pricing, minor gaps in risk management**

## Project Overview
Syncro proposes a dedicated web-based inventory dashboard tailored for NordFrame's 6 regional warehouses, building directly on your existing PostgreSQL database without schema changes.

## Scope of Work
1. **Real-Time Dashboard**: Web app displaying live stock levels across all 6 locations, connecting directly to your PostgreSQL DB.
2. **Low-Stock Alerts**: Automated email/SMS alerts to warehouse managers based on custom threshold rules.
3. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: HQ staff access all 6 warehouses; warehouse managers are restricted strictly to their assigned site.
4. **Site Onboarding**: Sequential onboarding plan (1 pilot warehouse, followed by 5 sites over 5 months).

## Timeline & Milestones
- **Month 2.5**: Pilot deployment at Warehouse 1.
- **Month 5.5**: Complete rollout across all 6 warehouses.
