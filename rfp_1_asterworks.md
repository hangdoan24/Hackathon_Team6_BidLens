# Request for Proposal — Predictive Maintenance Monitoring Platform

**Client:** AsterWorks Manufacturing GmbH (fictional)  
**Industry:** Industrial Manufacturing

## Background
AsterWorks operates three production plants in Germany. Unplanned machine downtime has increased during the last 12 months and maintenance teams currently rely on spreadsheets, alarm logs, and manual inspections.

## Requirements
1. A web-based maintenance dashboard showing machine status, active alarms, downtime history, and maintenance priority.
2. Integration with the existing Siemens S7 PLC environment and historian database.
3. **Existing PLC hardware must remain in place. No PLC replacement is permitted.**
4. The core monitoring solution must run **on-premise** because production-network data may not be sent to a public cloud.
5. A pilot covering one production line within **8 weeks**.
6. Rollout to all three plants within **5 months**.
7. Role-based access for operators, maintenance engineers, and plant managers.
8. A clear support model with incident response times after go-live.
9. Assumptions, dependencies, and implementation risks must be documented.

## Budget
€90,000–€130,000 including first-year support.

## Custom Evaluation Criteria
### Technical Compatibility
Does the proposal fit AsterWorks' existing Siemens S7, historian, and on-premise technical environment without forcing prohibited infrastructure changes?

### Delivery Feasibility
Are the proposed timeline, scope, resources, and commitments realistically achievable together?

## Decision Priority
Continuity of production is more important than introducing new technology for its own sake.
